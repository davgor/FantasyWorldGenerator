Biome review, 2026-09-20. Three cards, all octave-independent and all provable from source. [BESTIARY-LAND-ICE-NO-ANIMALS](backlog/BESTIARY-LAND-ICE-NO-ANIMALS.md) — biome 17 appears in 0 of 21 role tables, and `biome_weight`'s `table.get(str(id), 0.)` makes absent mean excluded, so all 311 animals score 0.0 on land ice while all 369 monsters fall through to 1.0: the ice is monster territory by accident and nineteen already-authored polar species score zero on the only biome that is theirs. The fix is eight numeric keys with weights already chosen, each strictly below that role's tundra weight; it is blocked on [PRODUCT-WORLD-DISPOSABILITY-DECISION](backlog/PRODUCT-WORLD-DISPOSABILITY-DECISION.md) alone, with no measurement behind the hold. [BIOME-TUNDRA-SNOW-UNREACHABLE](backlog/BIOME-TUNDRA-SNOW-UNREACHABLE.md) — `classify()` assigns tundra (1) and snow (6) and the ecology pass overwrites both in 8820 of 8820 swept cases, confirmed absent from all twelve worlds on disk, so two of the thirteen published biomes cannot exist in a finished world while `terrain.biome.001` and `.006` are still commissioned as art. Retirement is not a two-row delete: `VARIANT_NAMES` indexes `NATURAL_BIOMES` positionally and `biome_variant` is a flat index into the 156-entry cross product, so deleting row 1 makes index 1 the desert and renumbers every saved world — tombstoning is recommended and decouples this from disposability. The card also records desert as a near miss rather than a third case: it is absent below size 65 only because the grid cannot produce a dry cell, and anyone who had retuned its thresholds against a size-17 world would have broken warm worlds permanently. [BESTIARY-MONSTERS-BIOME-BLIND](backlog/BESTIARY-MONSTERS-BIOME-BLIND.md) — no monster carries a role or `biome_weights`, so biome identity contributes nothing to any of the 369; with only 42 carrying any relief weight, terrain steering is thin and biome steering is absent, and the behaviour arises from a fallback rather than a decision. Filed alongside them, [CONTENT-ARCHETYPE-FIELD-DOMAIN-MISMATCH](backlog/CONTENT-ARCHETYPE-FIELD-DOMAIN-MISMATCH.md) — five archetypes gate on a field that carries no data in the domain they declare. `salt_mine`, `salt_pans` and `whaling_station` are `domain: land` reading `salinity` and `fishing_productivity`, which are identically zero on every land cell and saturated on every water cell, so they are unplaceable by construction and no raster will change that. The structural rule the card states is the valuable part: an absolute floor on an empty field fails closed, but a percentile on an empty field fails OPEN — the cut computes to zero, every cell passes, and `holy_well` places with its defining requirement never enforced while reporting `placed`. That is the only defect in this sweep invisible in `diagnostics`, because it produces content that looks correct. Note an open reconciliation: the coordinator's run of `readers.cells` against `Artifacts/city-planner/world.json` returned 39 land cells with `island_habitat` and `freshwater_distance` nonzero on a few of them, where the card reports 47 and identically zero; the `salinity` and `fishing_productivity` result is confirmed in both.

Filed at the close of the sweep: [TIME-PERSISTED-WORLD-CANNOT-ADVANCE](backlog/TIME-PERSISTED-WORLD-CANNOT-ADVANCE.md) — `advance_age_request` on a CLI-written world raises `KeyError: 'timing_ms'` before doing any work, because `cli.py` strips timings deliberately to keep the document byte-reproducible while `terrain_biomes.py:100` writes into them unguarded. Proved by running both paths: `--include-timings` advances fine and is documented as not byte-reproducible, so a world is today either reproducible or advanceable and never both. The CLI settles the design question in passing — timings are “a profiling aid, not part of the interchange contract” — so the fix is every writer tolerating absence rather than a `setdefault` at whichever site is reached first. No test covers the seam because every test advances an in-memory world that still carries the key. Sequence with [TIME-ADVANCE](backlog/TIME-ADVANCE.md).
