# FantasyWorldGenerator ticket board

- `in-progress/` contains active work with recorded scope and evidence.
- `backlog/` contains reviewed, dependency-ordered work that is not yet active.
- `done/` contains imported historical LAB evidence and completed local tickets.

CASTLE-PLANNER is active: independent `castle_plans` modular fortification layouts from fortress pins ([CASTLE-PLANNER](in-progress/CASTLE-PLANNER.md)). ML-00–03 have concrete local tickets. ML-03 is the Unreal setup epic: (1) `.uplugin` + in-process native world generate, (2) UnrealWorldGen importer, (3) packaged generate → materialize. Slices: [ML-03d](backlog/ML-03d.md) then [ML-03e](backlog/ML-03e.md). ML-04–13 remain roadmap epics in `PLAN.md` and must be decomposed into bounded behavioral slices before implementation.

HERO-GUILD’s opt-in planning calculator is complete. ML-00, ML-01 (including a/b/c/d), ML-02 and the scoped ML-03a headless C++ proof are complete. ML-03 is active. ML-03b/c are complete. Native recipe-3 generate, detailed sampling, Unreal-axis fixtures and catalogue registry coverage are queued as [ML-03d](backlog/ML-03d.md). The UnrealWorldGen pass is [ML-03e](backlog/ML-03e.md): hub, registry placeholders, terrain contact, packaged Win64 digest. Decision 018 forbids a Python sidecar. Hosted CI/immutable channel may remain on parent ML-03 after that digest.

[ML-03f](backlog/ML-03f.md) closes what ML-03e left wrong once the generator changes in flight have landed: draw the dimensioned buildings the ported city geometry now emits, make the plugin folder a hermetic carryable artifact with a readable generator identity, fix the tectonic wavelength that never scales with the world (one octave of five survives at the 60 km cook, none at 200 km), and pay down the port hygiene each porting agent flagged rather than hid.

STORY-WEB is active: a separate `Sim/story_web/` package compiles every living hero's trope spokes, deterministic weights, offered hook, bound acts and exit threads into a `story_web` block beside `heroes`, rendered in the lab ([STORY-WEB](in-progress/STORY-WEB.md)).

KEY-LOCATIONS is complete: a separate `Sim/key_locations/` package places ninety-seven catalogue archetypes across eleven families as `(archetype, tier, state, occupant, origin)` — caves, mines, barrows, shrines, watchtowers, lairs, drowned ruins and wonders — with derived state and occupant, a succession pass that yields dungeons without a `dungeon` archetype, chains and clusters for the places that are wrong placed alone, and tier-2 interior chamber graphs, exported as `key_locations` and rendered in the lab ([KEY-LOCATIONS](done/KEY-LOCATIONS.md)).

SUPER-VILLAINS is active: the continuation of PLAN.md §21, which has been a heading with no body since extraction ([SUPER-VILLAINS](in-progress/SUPER-VILLAINS.md)). Two tiers of antagonist — world-scale villains that rise from recorded history and cause city fates in their own name, and a corrupted tier the four hidden schools seat only once a player exists. The shared rebuild tail, the `KNOWN_SCHOOLS` seam and the biome catalogue restructure have landed as no-op prerequisites; appending the hidden schools is the retiring change and has not.

NOMADS is complete: travelling groups placed after the simulation settles, classified by the ground they start on rather than by a free roll ([NOMADS](done/NOMADS.md)). Six kinds — bandits, cultists, deserters, merchants, survivors and wanderers — each behind a hard geographic gate, so a cult needs charged ground and a god bound to its school, a caravan needs a road and two markets, and a candidate satisfying no gate raises nobody. Routes follow from the classification: a two-move seasonal round for herders, a market chain subdivided by day marches, a closed circuit over one god's sacred ground, a lair with raiding spurs. The creature catalogue gained five movement classes on the same footing — an undead with a grave nests at it, an undead without one wanders — exported as `beast_movements`, with an `encounters` index over both so a future quest handler can ask what is near here and when.
