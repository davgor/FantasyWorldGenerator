# FantasyWorldGenerator ticket board

- `in-progress/` contains active work with recorded scope and evidence.
- `backlog/` contains reviewed, dependency-ordered work that is not yet active.
- `done/` contains imported historical LAB evidence and completed local tickets.

STORY-WEB is active: a separate `Sim/story_web/` package compiles every living hero's trope spokes, deterministic weights, offered hook, bound acts and exit threads into a `story_web` block beside `heroes`, rendered in the lab ([STORY-WEB](in-progress/STORY-WEB.md)).

KEY-LOCATIONS is complete: a separate `Sim/key_locations/` package places ninety-seven catalogue archetypes across eleven families as `(archetype, tier, state, occupant, origin)` — caves, mines, barrows, shrines, watchtowers, lairs, drowned ruins and wonders — with derived state and occupant, a succession pass that yields dungeons without a `dungeon` archetype, chains and clusters for the places that are wrong placed alone, and tier-2 interior chamber graphs, exported as `key_locations` and rendered in the lab ([KEY-LOCATIONS](done/KEY-LOCATIONS.md)).

SUPER-VILLAINS is active: the continuation of PLAN.md §21, which has been a heading with no body since extraction ([SUPER-VILLAINS](in-progress/SUPER-VILLAINS.md)). Two tiers of antagonist — world-scale villains that rise from recorded history and cause city fates in their own name, and a corrupted tier the four hidden schools seat only once a player exists. The shared rebuild tail, the `KNOWN_SCHOOLS` seam and the biome catalogue restructure have landed as no-op prerequisites; appending the hidden schools is the retiring change and has not.

NOMADS is complete: travelling groups placed after the simulation settles, classified by the ground they start on rather than by a free roll ([NOMADS](done/NOMADS.md)). Six kinds — bandits, cultists, deserters, merchants, survivors and wanderers — each behind a hard geographic gate, so a cult needs charged ground and a god bound to its school, a caravan needs a road and two markets, and a candidate satisfying no gate raises nobody. Routes follow from the classification: a two-move seasonal round for herders, a market chain subdivided by day marches, a closed circuit over one god's sacred ground, a lair with raiding spurs. The creature catalogue gained five movement classes on the same footing — an undead with a grave nests at it, an undead without one wanders — exported as `beast_movements`, with an `encounters` index over both so a future quest handler can ask what is near here and when.

Adversarial review, 2026-09-20, against `233182e`. Six red-team lenses ran in parallel; findings are filed as cards below and as reviews under [docs/reviews/](../docs/reviews/). The blocking one is [PRINCIPAL-GRID-BOUND-PARITY](backlog/PRINCIPAL-GRID-BOUND-PARITY.md) — a grid ceiling widened from 257 to 1025 at the Python entry points and carried to no consumer, which leaves `validate_repo --stage repo-tests` non-terminating for every session. **Ruled 2026-09-20: 1025 is correct.** The reference path was right; `terrain_history.py:590` (age advance), `Core/genesis.hpp:8` (`max_grid`, thrown at `genesis.cpp:92`) and `Core/README.md:75` widen to match, as one change with its native port. The 257 in `tools/terrain_lab.py` is the deliberate interactive limit stated at [terrain-math-lab.md](../docs/terrain-math-lab.md) and stays; `terrain_patch.py:89` and `height-tile.schema.json` are undecided and named in the ticket. The ruling is load-bearing rather than cosmetic: the octave filter at `terrain_tectonics.py:178` admits 4 of 5 octaves at 257 and all 5 at 513, so until now a world could carry all of its terrain or all of its history and never both — the seed-42 size-17 reference world every suite pins to resolves 0 of 5 and has no surface noise at all. [PRODUCT-CAPABILITY-RANGE-DIVERGENCE](backlog/PRODUCT-CAPABILITY-RANGE-DIVERGENCE.md) follows it: every world document publishes a parameter registry advertising a grid range four times wider than any native consumer accepts. [PRODUCT-WORLD-DISPOSABILITY-DECISION](backlog/PRODUCT-WORLD-DISPOSABILITY-DECISION.md) — eleven documents require every existing world to be regenerated and no ticket owns the consequence; needs a ruling on whether worlds are disposable until a named milestone. [PRODUCT-BLOCK-REGISTRY](backlog/PRODUCT-BLOCK-REGISTRY.md) — 48 published blocks, 30 with neither an envelope declaration nor a schema, and a conformance test that passes on a world missing 38 of them; sequence with [VILLAINS-NO-SCHEMA](backlog/VILLAINS-NO-SCHEMA.md), whose policy question this generalises. [PRODUCT-CONSUMER-VOCABULARY](backlog/PRODUCT-CONSUMER-VOCABULARY.md) — `tier` means four things including a float that becomes metres, and `status` names two different axes at two depths inside one block. [PRODUCT-CONFORMANCE-PROOF-UNCHECKED](backlog/PRODUCT-CONFORMANCE-PROOF-UNCHECKED.md) — eight of nine conformance record keys are read by nothing; cheapest to fix now while only one record exists. [PRODUCT-RUNTIME-PARITY-LEDGER](backlog/PRODUCT-RUNTIME-PARITY-LEDGER.md) — the board's “complete” has detached from PLAN.md's “runtime-supported” and nothing states the native gap as a number.
