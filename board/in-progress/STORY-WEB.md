# STORY-WEB — Deterministic story webs for every living hero

## Requested behavior

The user wants story cards as an optional tool for the orchestrator: quest templates (literary tropes) that are dynamically instantiated around the heroes the hero generator precipitates, so that a playwright-style orchestrator can plan complex stories from very little data. Each hero gets a web: the tropes their history admits, arranged like a spiderweb (angle is alignment, rings are acts, the rim is the next rest), with the offered hook on the heaviest spoke. Assignment must be deterministic and weight-based, never an LLM. Most of the web is precompiled after the cast is created so the runtime orchestrator is a lookup, not an agent. The webs must be visible on the terrain lab page. Before calling it done, review the generated characters against their logs and tweak the equations until the assignment reads right. Theory and data model: this session's design conversation (2026-09-19), summarised in `docs/story-web.md`.

## Proposed mechanism

A wholly separate package, `Sim/story_web/`, called once right after `_attach_heroes` with the finished world. For every living person and Dread it derives facts from the exported record and a few world blocks, evaluates every trope's entry predicate (any-of-conjunctions, the archetype card shape, with `!` negation), weights the eligible spokes by `boosts × alignment_fit × archetype_fit × claim_fit`, offers the heaviest as the resting hook (ties on trope id), binds the offered trope's three acts and options to the hero's names, and lists the threads each act can exit along with one typed condition each and any cut the archetype's typed constraints impose. Forty-six tropes ship as data in `policies/tropes.json`; constraints and the weight constants are data too. Outcomes are not pre-recorded: each act has a completion predicate, an abandonment deadline and examples in prose.

## Dependencies and unresolved decisions

- Consumes the `heroes` block from HERO-GENERATOR (slices 1–3 as present in the working tree on 2026-09-19); embeds a cast in its fixture so a cast policy edit cannot silently move this package's pin.
- Not in scope: the runtime walker (firing hooks, applying option deltas, resolving abandonment offstage), the player's own person record, the deeds well that turns accepted events into features, and any voice adapter. The block is their compiled input.
- Decided: no draw decides an assignment; the seed only staggers initiative deadlines. Decided: the enable switch is an environment variable like the cast's.
- Open: world facts are coarse (a lunar surge anywhere counts for every magic-adjacent person; reach is the home city). Tropes whose every entry needs a feature nothing computes (person-shaped Dreads, lost allies, two-school deeds) are exported as unreachable; with the current catalogue none is, since the cities well also precipitates prophets, heresiarchs, exiles and founders from diaspora cities.

## Acceptance and evidence

- Every living hero is woven or reported with the nearest misses; offered is the heaviest spoke; armed follow in order; three acts each with two to four options; threads never point at the offered spoke; dreads walk only dread spokes.
- Replay byte-identical; source world untouched; JSON finite; a different seed changes only the initiative stagger.
- Weight equation: alignment fit monotonic in the pull and floored; archetype and claim fits multiply; breakdown reproduces the number; angle convention pinned.
- Constraints cut threads: an Obsessed Hunter's claim threads, an Oathbound's claim threads, an Incarnate's alignment threads.
- Catalogue lint: exactly three acts, bounded deltas, known facts only, every archetype fits at least one spoke, no silent act for people.
- Pinned fixture (cast embedded): summary, offered, spokes with weights, first-act threads, unwoven.
- `attach` returns a failed block on a missing or failed cast and `None` when switched off; the package never imports `icarus_sim` or `hero_generator`.
- Generated world (conformance suite): `story_web` present, valid against the schema, hidden before stage 16, every offered trope in the catalogue and every web uid a living hero.
- Lab: story-web panel renders webs, histogram, acts and truth details; atlas hover names the offered spoke (built-in browser screenshot).
- Calibration: `tools/story_web_report.py` over the fixture cast and a generated world; flags reviewed and the equation tuned; findings recorded below.

## Adversarial review and limitations

Opus red team (2026-09-19) over the whole diff: no blockers; ten should-fixes, all applied. (1) Thread compilation only inspected the nearest entry conjunction and dropped threads whose other conjunction was gainable: every conjunction is now tried and the cheapest way in kept. (2) The unreachable-trope list wrongly named `role:schemer`, `stakes:2`, `routes:2` and `realm:cold_conflicts_2`, which the cities well does emit, and on a second look also the diaspora roles: corrected (`NEVER_COMPUTED` is now only person-shaped Dreads, lost allies and two-school deeds), no trope is unreachable, docs fixed. (3) The exported breakdown did not reproduce the weight after rounding: the weight is now computed from the rounded parts and a test asserts reproduction over every spoke. (4) Thread weights were pre-change: they are now the weight after the condition holds. (5) The cap cut immediately walkable threads: threads rank open, alignment, claim, feature, then weight. (6) Opposition was measured against the pole threshold instead of the pull: it is now the sign of the dot product, with the hardest-pulling axis reported. (7) Threads were one aliased list per act and options/completion objects were the policy document's own: threads are hoisted to the web and policy sub-objects are copied. (8) Bind could raise on carried relics or nameless records: guarded. (9) Six world facts were never exercised by the unit tests: a synthetic threat/food/moon/mantle test covers them, plus report and bind tests. (10) Tied spokes drew on top of each other in the lab: fanned by a few degrees. Nits taken: negated tokens are not gainable, dead reachability branch removed, the CRLF rewrite of the two lab files reverted so the diff is 4 and 175 lines, the histogram counts only visible webs. A second red-team pass re-verified every fix against the final tree (0 breakdown mismatches over 93 spokes, 0 dropped open threads, byte-identical under three hash seeds) and disputed the diaspora correction; that dispute was settled empirically (a hand-built world with a schism-founded city yields a prophet carrying `role:prophet`, and seed 42 simply has no diaspora cities) and pinned by a test that generates such a cast and checks `NEVER_COMPUTED` against the features actually emitted. Its two remaining asks were taken: the world-fact reads in `facts.py` skip malformed rows instead of failing the block, and the pinned fixture world now carries threat assessments, a food deficit, almanac events and an open mantle so the world facts move pinned bytes. Limitations: compiled offer only, coarse world facts, unreachable tropes listed not hidden, no runtime state; the lab's legacy summon and stage code still logs console errors when rendering a stage-less CLI export, which predates this change.

## Follow-up — the Siege density (2026-09-19)

The Siege held 9 of 56 offers on seed 42 although not one living city had a living former opponent: every war ruins its loser, and `war_pressure` (0.15 per war ever fought, never decaying) put five cities' sovereigns and warlords on a defence against nobody. Six changes, all taken:

1. **Threat assessment 3** (`terrain_history.assess_city_threat`, `terrain_wars.war_outlook`): beside the backward pressure, a forward outlook per city: `wars_recent`, `enemy_living`, `war_risk` (the strongest contention pressure a living neighbour exerts, with kind and opponent) and `war_hunger` (the pressure the city exerts on a road neighbour it cannot out-feed; a supply-pressed pair is directed, the hungrier city carries the hunger and the other the risk). `regional_threat` is unchanged so fortification does not move. `Sim/tests/test_war_outlook.py` covers it; `test_threat_assessments` pins version 3 and the fields.
2. **Time-scoped facts** in the story web: `world:war_recent`, `world:enemy_living`, `world:war_risk`, `world:war_hunger`, `world:famine` (seasonal food coverage below `weights.famine_coverage`, 0.5, instead of the structural deficit that marks 8 of 9 cores) and person facts `war:veteran`, `war:victor_recent`, `war:defeated_recent` against the cast's final age. The Siege now needs `war_risk` or `enemy_living` (or a pressed fortress); the chronic pressure only arms threads. The Famine needs `world:famine` or a starving hamlet.
3. **Post-war spokes** for victors: The Spoils (the taken ground and its heir), The Veteran (the army after the war), The Peace (terms before the next one), The Pursuit (the warlord's side of the heir's Revenge), and The March (the hungry city that will attack). Tropes revision 7, 51 tropes.
4. **The Siege re-aimed**: sovereign and warlord role boosts dropped; commander, castellan and champion hold it.
5. **Wars may leave survivors**: world option `war_survival` (registry `Communities`, default 0 so every seed replays byte for byte; mirrored in `Core/config.hpp`, not yet drawn natively). Above zero, a defeated city stands as its victor's vassal (`outcome: vassalage`, `survival_chance`, `survival_roll` on the war; `vassal_of` on the city; no fate), which is what makes `enemy_living` and `realm:contested` possible. `test_war_outlook.WarSurvivalTests` covers zero-identity, vassalage and replay.
6. **Cast mix**: warlord base 0.35 → 0.25 and `one_per_city` (only the most recent victor per city precipitates); wells revision 5, hero fixture re-pinned.
