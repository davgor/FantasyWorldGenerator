# LAB-017 — Terrain city planning is profile-driven and terrain-adaptive

Status: done. Owner/session: MathLab city planning follow-up, consolidated on main.

## Scope and acceptance

Derive each city layout from a deterministic, data-driven building pack and layout profile. Plans expose leaders' homes, barracks, noble and worker housing, apartments, market districts, optional religious buildings, and river-fork bridge recommendations. Feature anchors must remain on land, within the profile's slope limit and outside its river buffer; insufficient valid terrain produces explicit missing-anchor evidence rather than silently relaxing constraints.
