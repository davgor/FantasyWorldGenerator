## Other generator outputs

- `height`, `slope`, `tpi`, landform and erosion/deposition: MAT/GEO selections and placement gates; terrain mesh itself is epic 009 output.
- `water_type`, surface height, freshwater distance, river edges: WATER and relevant VEG/PROP families; water depth may require derived engine geometry.
- Temperature/moisture: ground/vegetation eligibility and seasonal variants. Seasonal snowfall/rain visuals require runtime hooks; a mean field alone is not live weather.
- Magic density/growth/hazard and ley nodes: FUNG, crystal GEO, enchanted/haunted TREE/VEG and FX. No protective ward gameplay is implied by a prop.
- Resource potential: generic resource-depot and stockpile props; named ore types require extra simulation.
- City/hamlet/fortress/wizard-college kind: ARCH assemblies for human/dwarf/elf; BLD supplies components.
- Population profile: PPL/ROLE and architecture family; culture ID chooses an eligible style variation.
- Rural farming/resource role: field/crop/storage/tool PROP jobs; role people use existing allocations.
- Roads/crossings: road, bridge, culvert, dock requirements. Actual engineered placement and shipping are separate integration work.
- Food/storage/transport ledgers: visual inventory states for stores, sacks, carts and work stations; no free food added by art.
- Seed/world size: separate deterministic asset-choice stream and physical dimensions, not a new per-world asset library.

## Nonvisual or future inputs

Plate velocities, solver residuals, timing metrics, support allowances and cost graphs primarily drive simulation/debugging; no dedicated art asset is required. Exact botanical species, geological minerals, live storms, active volcano state, dwelling occupancy and historical ruins are not fully represented by current fields. Their selectors remain explicitly deferred until epic 009 or later simulation supplies the data.
