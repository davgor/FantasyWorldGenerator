Scope: catalogue profiles, nest generation and atlas against docs/terrain-world-layers.md and docs/catalogue/README.md. Self-review; no independent reviewer.

Resolved findings:
- Initial profile preparation misclassified several terrestrial candidates through ambiguous prose (season, disease, seal). Reviewed aquatic assignments and explicitly corrected newt, spoonbill, corpse bear, gate colossus, folded leviathan, salt colossus and flounder habitats. Runtime reads explicit data, never interprets prose.
- UTF-8 candidate names now use the canonical catalogue encoding; tropical primates and selected reptiles received warm climate bounds.
- Cinder dragons, magma salamanders and volcano hearts now require a volcanic field; cliff specialists require mountain relief instead of relying solely on a soft preference.
- Preserved phase/recipe gates, upstream RNG isolation, separate sky layer addressing and finite export. Per-species spacing uses spherical distance; global budget and one-per-cell selection cannot violate that spacing.

Evidence: six new behavioral tests (first failed before implementation); complete 110 terrain and 19 tooling tests passed again after final profile refinements; preservation and documentation validation passed. JavaScript syntax passed. Live 129-grid preview showed 120 anchors, absent-species diagnostics, glacier-dragon requirements and a wyvern on sky-2-2. Browser interaction confirmed species inspection.

Limits: first-pass artistic profiles, bounded sampling, no ecological carrying capacity, hostility, migration, cave geometry or Unreal actors. Territories may overlap between species. Sky anchors currently use one center candidate per island. Live candidate catalogue remains unchanged. No claim of real biological accuracy or packaged-engine behavior.
